👨‍🚀 How quotes work

Spectrum's public API publishes prices and volumes per market — not pool reserves. AstroErg estimates reserves as ~10× reported volume, split 50/50 by value at the spot price, then runs your trade through x·y=k with the 0.5% Spectrum-style pool fee. Price impact is computed against that estimated depth, so treat it as indicative. Token→token routes go via ERG in two legs and say so.
